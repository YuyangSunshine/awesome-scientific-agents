# Taxonomy

[← Home](../README.md)

The seven lists preserve the source library's six work packages plus its survey collection. Membership is thematic and can overlap. The survey list preserves the original survey collection; other reviews and perspectives also appear in their subject lists.

| ID | Topic | Selection focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ROR | Surveys and perspectives | Existing landscape coverage and framework context |
| WP1 | Scientific agents | AI scientists, hypothesis generation and research workflows |
| WP2 | Autonomous laboratories | Physical execution and self-driving experimental platforms |
| WP3 | Scientific evidence | Reasoning, validation, reproducibility and claim support |
| WP4 | Experiment design | Active learning, Bayesian optimization and experiment selection |
| WP5 | Continual learning | Knowledge updates, model/policy updates and capability improvement; relevant background methods |
| WP6 | Benchmarks | Evaluation tasks, constructs and scientific capability boundaries |

Three analytical questions connect the lists: **control** (who can decide and authorize), **evidence** (what supports the claim), and **learning** (what changes and whether it helps). These questions are reading lenses, not numerical scores or a completed evidence classification.
